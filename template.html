How to report your progress

  1. Keep Label Studio running (./start.sh) and, after a labelling session, open your kit folder.
  2. Double-click update.command (Mac) or run ./update.sh (Windows WSL / Linux). It sends your counts only, never products or labels.
  3. This page rebuilds by itself about a minute later.
  4. If it says it could not send, run .venv/bin/python kit.py progress and send the SHOWME-S2 line to the owner.
